WHAT IS THIS?

A commons is something
we make together.

Solarpunk Commons is an open experiment in collective imagination and practical collaboration. It connects people, projects, skills and questions that might otherwise remain scattered.

You don't need a finished project or a grand plan. Curiosity is a good place to start.
