WHAT IS THIS?

A commons is something
we make together.

Solarpunk Commons is an open experiment in collective imagination and practical collaboration. It connects people, projects, places, skills, offers and questions that might otherwise remain scattered.

It is not about collecting followers or building another feed. It is about making useful connections: finding somewhere to go, someone to learn from, a project to join, a place to stay, or people to build something with.

You don't need a finished project or a grand plan. Curiosity is a good place to start.
