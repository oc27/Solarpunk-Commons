06 — Keep the commons healthy

How to contribute well

Good collective intelligence depends on trust, context and useful evidence. A few principles for everything added here.

01 / Grounded

Show your working

Link to sources, name the place and date, distinguish first-hand experience from interpretation, and flag what is uncertain.

02 / Generous

Make it reusable

Describe what someone else could adapt. Share failures and limitations as well as successes; avoid presenting one model as universal.

03 / Accountable

Credit the work

Respect community knowledge, ask before sharing sensitive details, credit contributors and keep local communities in control of their stories.
