ABOUT THE RESEARCH

What is Solarpunk Commons?

This is an online portal for people that want to join a distributed research project. Launched by the London School of Solarpunk, the purpose is to create a global community to share resources and opportunities.

02 / THE RATIONALE

Why are we doing this?

Our purpose is to evolve the solarpunk movement. This research project will achieve this by connecting people together and exploring important questions that will shape the future of the movement.

03 / THE QUESTIONS

What are we researching?

We want to provide a research directory of people involved in solarpunk activities. We want to pose questions that can help new projects and experiments.

04 / PARTICIPATION

How does it work?

This is phase one of the project. We want people that are interested to build their co-researcher profile and answer the questions above. Answers will be linked to profiles so users of this site can easily explore the people involved.
